Reproducing the paper

The scripts under scripts/ and the dACESwarms subpackage, which simulates collectives with known interaction rules, produce the dACE content of every figure, table and number of the paper. All run from the repository root with the scripts/paper environment, which the root project covers as a Julia workspace:

julia --project=. -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.instantiate()'

Benchmarks

julia -t auto --project=scripts/paper scripts/paper/figures.jl      # fits, Figures 4–6, Table 1, numbers
julia --project=scripts/paper scripts/paper/fig_recovery.jl         # Figure 3 (without SocialNet), from the results of figures.jl
julia --project=scripts/paper scripts/paper/fig_schematic.jl        # Figure 1
julia --project=scripts/paper scripts/paper/fig_data.jl             # Figure 2
julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/paper/timing.jl          # fit and evaluation times, for reference (macOS only)
julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/synthetic/chiral.jl      # then fig_chiral.jl: Figure 7
julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/synthetic/efficiency.jl  # then fig_efficiency.jl: Figure 8
julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/synthetic/stochastic.jl  # then fig_selfpropulsion.jl: Figure 9

Figures go to scripts/paper/figures/, or to a directory passed as the first argument; the scripts in scripts/synthetic and scripts/realdata write their results to a results/ folder next to them, which the figure scripts read. scripts/paper/replot.jl redraws every figure from cached data and results without refitting.

The comparison with SocialNet in the paper uses the authors' code and is not part of this repository. The rollout videos of the paper are on figshare, 10.6084/m9.figshare.34831176.

Tracked fish

The tracking data are not part of the repository. Put them under data/ (or the directory in DACE_DATA):

  • data/shiners_dryad/: the golden shiner trajectories of Davidson et al. (2021), the unpacked Dryad archive 10.5061/dryad.sbcc2fr2h, one folder per trial; compact_shiners() in scripts/realdata/shiners.jl compacts it once to data/shiners/<trial>.h5.
  • data/zebrafish/<video>.h5: the zebrafish videos of Heras et al. (2019) in the human-validated idtracker.ai tracking of Torrents et al. (2026), the zebrafish_<fish>_<video>.npy files of the benchmark data linked from idtracker.ai (Google Drive), converted by scripts/realdata/convert_zebrafish.py. The original tracking of the 2019 paper (Google Drive) goes to data/zebrafish2019/ as the second tracking of the cross-tracking test.
SHINER_GAP=0 julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/realdata/shiners_protocol.jl         # leave one trial out; Figure 11 at gap 0
ZEBRAFISH_GAP=0 julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/realdata/zebrafish_protocol.jl    # leave one video out, cross-tracking
CONSISTENCY_DATA=shiners julia -t 5 --project=scripts/paper scripts/realdata/consistency.jl   # and zebrafish; then fig_realdata.jl: Figures 10, 11

SHINER_GAP and ZEBRAFISH_GAP take the gaps of the paper (0, 3, 6 frames); SHINER_R and ZEBRAFISH_R the cutoffs in body lengths (defaults 3 and 8).

Installing the simulator separately

using Pkg
Pkg.add(url="https://github.com/TSGut/dACE.jl", subdir="lib/dACESwarms")

Tests

julia --project=. -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.test()'
julia --project=lib/dACESwarms -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.test()'
